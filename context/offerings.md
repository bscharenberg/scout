# What BTO offers

BTO Group has four practice areas. Each has one flagship offering that most first engagements start from.

The idea behind all of them is the Ground Truth approach: every engagement starts with an unfiltered look at what is actually going on before any recommendation. Most consultancies arrive with an answer and look for evidence. BTO finds the answer in the evidence. BTO also builds the answer with the client's own people, so they own it after BTO leaves.

## Organizational Strategy

Helps leadership teams make the big directional calls: where to play, how to win, and how to get the organization behind a clear agenda.

Typical triggers: a new CEO or leadership change, stalled growth or margin pressure with no clear cause, a board or PE sponsor asking for strategic clarity, a messy portfolio after acquisitions.

Flagship: **Ground Truth Sprint.** Six weeks, fixed fee. A current-state assessment, a prioritized agenda, and an agreed point of view on the three to five decisions that matter most. The deliverable is a decision, not a document.

## Operating Model Design

Helps organizations change how they run: structure, who is accountable for what, who decides what, and ways of working.

Typical triggers: a merger that needs structural alignment, fast growth straining the organization, a cost mandate with performance expectations attached, new digital capabilities that don't fit the old structure.

Flagship: **Operating Model Blueprint.** Ten to fourteen weeks. Current-state diagnosis, design options, a leadership alignment workshop, and an implementation roadmap with a decision rights framework. Implementation detail is built in from the first week, because org redesigns usually fail on decision rights and on not involving the people who have to live in the new model.

## Digital and Data Transformation

Helps organizations set and run technology and data strategy. BTO does the strategy, governance, and operating model that make a digital program work. It does not implement technology.

Typical triggers: a digital program that stalled with no clear owner, data scattered across business units, a new CDO or CTO who needs a credible roadmap, board pressure for a coherent digital agenda.

Flagship: **Digital Readiness Assessment and Roadmap.** Eight weeks. A prioritized roadmap, a capability gap analysis, a governance model, and a sequenced investment plan. Because BTO earns nothing from implementation, it has no platform bias. The client picks the implementer afterward, without BTO in the room.

## M&A Integration

Helps acquirers plan and run the integration of acquired companies, especially the operating model, culture, and capability decisions that decide whether a deal delivers.

Typical triggers: a completed or upcoming acquisition, a PE buy-and-build strategy, past acquisitions that missed their targets, an integration team that is overwhelmed or doesn't exist yet.

Flagship: **Integration Accelerator.** Starts before close and runs through the 100-day and 12-month marks. Covers setting up the integration office, tracking synergies, operating model decisions, and a culture alignment framework. A light pre-close planning sprint is available when the deal hasn't closed yet.

## Entry points and deal sizes

- A diagnostic sprint of $75K to $120K is the usual way to start a first engagement with a cautious buyer. It almost always leads to a second phase when it goes well.
- Mid-market engagements usually run $150K to $600K.
- Enterprise engagements usually run $800K to $3M or more, often over several phases.
